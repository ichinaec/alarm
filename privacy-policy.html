Chronaly · 时光纪Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

ENGLISH

1.Overview

Chronaly is an on-device reminder tool (reminders, countdowns, habits, quick timers, voice input). This policy explains how we handle privacy-related information. Chronaly is a fully standalone app: no accounts, no cloud server — all your data stays on your device by default.

Effective date: Jan 1, 2026 · Version: v1.0

2.What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect any personal identity information (no registration, no phone number, no email);
  • We do not use any advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking SDKs (no IDFA / advertising identifier);
  • We do not upload your reminders, habit records, or countdown data — they are stored only in the local on-device database.

3.Permissions

  • Notifications: delivering reminders at times you set — the core function of the app;
  • Microphone: records audio only while you actively use "voice input" to create a reminder. Audio is used solely to produce the reminder text and is discarded immediately after recognition;
  • Calendar: only if you enable "calendar mirroring", the app creates a sub-calendar named "Chronaly Reminders" in your own system calendar as a backup delivery channel. The app cannot read anything else in your calendar;
  • Siri & Shortcuts: supports voice commands such as "start a countdown with Chronaly". Related data never leaves the device;
  • Background audio: keeps the countdown ringtone playing (including on the lock screen) until you manually stop it.

4.Speech Recognition

The voice feature converts your speech into reminder content. Audio and recognition serve only that single creation event and are discarded immediately afterward — never stored, never used for model training or any other purpose.

5.In-App Purchases

Pro subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely through Apple's In-App Purchase system. All payment information is handled by Apple; the developer never sees your card number, billing address, or any payment details. No transaction records are retained.

6.Data Storage & Deletion

  • All data (reminders, habits, countdowns, settings) lives in the local on-device database;
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data — nothing remains in any cloud;
  • Calendar mirror events can be cleared in one tap inside the app, or deleted directly in the system Calendar app.

7.Children's Privacy

The app is rated 4+ and performs no targeted collection from children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, it is equally safe for children.

8.Changes & Contact

Material changes to this policy will be announced in-app and on this page with an updated version number. Questions? Contact: support@chronaly.app (placeholder — replace before submission)

简体中文

1.总则

时光纪（Chronaly）是一款在设备本地运行的定时提醒工具（提醒 / 倒数日 / 打卡 / 快捷倒计时 / 语音创建）。本政策说明我们如何处理与您的隐私相关的信息。本应用为单机应用：无账号体系、无云服务器，您的全部数据默认存储在设备本地。

生效日期：2026 年 1 月 1 日 · 版本：v1.0

2.我们不收集的数据

  • 不收集任何个人身份信息（无需注册、无需手机号、无需邮箱）；
  • 不使用任何广告、统计或用户行为分析 SDK，不进行任何形式的跟踪（无 IDFA / 广告标识符读取）；
  • 不上传您的提醒内容、打卡记录、倒数日数据——它们仅保存在本机数据库中。

3.权限使用说明

  • 通知权限：用于在您设定的时间送达提醒，这是应用的核心功能；
  • 麦克风权限：仅在您主动使用"语音创建提醒"时录音，音频仅用于识别为提醒文字，识别完成即丢弃，绝不用于其他目的；
  • 日历权限：仅当您开启"日历提醒镜像"后，应用会在您自己的系统日历中创建名为"时光纪提醒"的子日历作为第三方提醒通道，应用无法读取您日历中的其他内容；
  • Siri 与快捷指令：用于支持"用时光纪设置倒计时"等语音命令，相关数据不出设备；
  • 后台音频：仅在倒计时结束响铃期间保持铃声播放（含锁屏状态），直到您手动停止。

4.语音识别

语音创建功能将您说出的话语转换为提醒内容。音频与识别过程仅服务于本次提醒创建，识别完成后立即处理丢弃，不会留存、不会用于模型训练或任何其他用途。

5.内购与付款

应用的 Pro 订阅与买断项目通过 Apple App Store 内购（IAP）完成。所有支付信息均由 Apple 处理，开发者无法接触您的卡号、账单地址等任何支付信息。我们不保存任何交易明细。

6.数据的存储与删除

  • 全部数据（提醒、打卡、倒数日、设置）存储于设备本地数据库；
  • 卸载应用即彻底删除全部数据，无任何云端残留；
  • 日历镜像产生的日程可在应用内一键清除，或直接在系统日历中删除。

7.儿童隐私

本应用内容分级为 4+，不面向 13 岁以下儿童做任何定向收集。由于不收集任何数据，儿童使用同样安全。

8.政策变更与联系

如政策有重大变更，将在应用内及本页面更新并更新顶部版本号。如有疑问，请联系：support@chronaly.app（占位，上线前替换）
